

**Original loss**

$$
L = L_{rate} + L_{edit} = ∑_{j=1}^{L} (λ_{ins,j} + λ_{del,j} + λ_{sub,j}) − w ∑_{e∈𝓔} log (u_e)
$$

Where for each event \(e\) at position \(j_e\):

\[
u_e =
\begin{cases}
\lambda_{\text{del}, j_e}                                     & \text{if \(e\) is a deletion}, \\[6pt]
\lambda_{\text{sub}, j_e}\, Q_{\text{sub}, j_e}(y_e)          & \text{if \(e\) is a substitution}, \\[6pt]
\lambda_{\text{ins}, j_e}\, Q_{\text{ins}, j_e}(y_e)          & \text{if \(e\) is an insertion}.
\end{cases}
\]


----------------------------------------------------------

**Reparametrized loss**

$λ_{tot, j} = \text{total edit rate for position j} = λ_{del,j} + λ_{sub,j} + λ_{ins,j}$

$\pi_{j}(\text{ins / sub / del}) = \text{distribution of edit types at position j} = \displaystyle\frac{λ_{del/ins/sub,j}}{λ_{tot,j}}$

$$
L = L_{\text{rate}} + L_{\text{edit}} = \gamma_{\text{rate}} [\sum_{j=1}^{L} \lambda_{\text{tot},j} - w \sum_{e \in \mathcal{E}} \log(\lambda_{\text{tot}, j_e})] + \gamma_{\text{edit}}
[- w \sum_{e \in \mathcal{E}} \log(v_e)]
$$


Where for each event \(e\) at position \(j_e\):

\[
v_e =
\begin{cases}
\pi_{, j_e}(\text{DEL})                              & \text{if \(e\) is a deletion}, \\[6pt]
\pi_{j_e}(\text{SUB}) \, Q_{\text{sub}, j_e}(y_e)  & \text{if \(e\) is a substitution}, \\[6pt]
\pi_{j_e}(\text{INS}) \, Q_{\text{ins}, j_e}(y_e)  & \text{if \(e\) is an insertion}.
\end{cases}
\]
